## 1. The cover shape, brought forward (B11.4, first part)

### 1.1 Goal & scope

**Why.** On a computer the cover is a 1024×320 band, 3.2:1 (measured on `main` and #737). An upright 3:4 portrait shows 23.4% of its height there, 15.3%–38.8% at 20% (arithmetic, matched by a pixel read within 0.1 point on 48 boxes). An upright room cut at 20% in that band shows its ceiling: `salon_excellence` 15.8%–36.7%, `barber_shop_pro` 16.1%–35.7%, `spa_relax` 15.0%–40.0%. The owner rejected ceilings on the card (BACKLOG 89), and on 2026-11-05 brought B11.4's better shape forward rather than keep 20% in that band (BACKLOG 94).

**What.** The salon's cover becomes 3:2 at every width, through an `aspect-cover` token, and keeps the cards' cut, `object-subject` (50% 20%). From `lg` the page takes the direction's §7.6 layout, which the shape needs: the cover in columns 1–8, and beside it the name, the rating and `BookingPanel`, with only the panel sticky. The phone keeps its order. In a 3:2 box a 3:4 portrait shows 50% of its height (10%–60%) and `salon_excellence` 44.4% (11.1%–55.6%), about twice today's share on a computer.

**User stories.**
- *Phone.* I open a salon's page. I see its photo in about the shape the app uses (the app's cover is about 360×248, 1.45:1), then the name, the rating and « Réserver » without scrolling.
- *Computer.* I see a large photo of the salon on the left. Beside it I see the name, the rating, the starting price and « Réserver ». While I read the services and reviews, the booking box stays in view.

**In scope, brought forward from B11.4.**
- The `cover` aspect token, 3:2.
- The 3:2 cover at every width.
- Direction §7.6's layout from `lg`: the site's container, columns 1–8 and 9–12, the sticky panel.
- The hero's « Réserver » hidden from `lg`, where the panel's is beside it.
- Exact `sizes` for the cover.

**In scope because this slice redraws those exact elements.**
1. **The favourite button's focus jump, a defect fix.** On load the `[busy]` effect of `FavoriteButton.tsx:45-49` focuses the heart with nothing focused, and the browser scrolls to it. On the live site's code (`main` `ea0c8f0c`) and on #737 the page lands scrolled 317 px at 320×568 and 454 px at 844×390, past the cover (measured; the file is the same in both).
2. **« Réserver » disabled in the owner's preview.** `ProviderView.tsx:138` passes no `preview` to the hero, so its « Réserver » is a live link in the preview (read in code; UNVERIFIED in a browser). The panel's and the bar's are already disabled there.

### 2.3 The message rule (`Toast`), precisely

**Who is measured.** A `Toast` given `aboveFloatingControl` (« Carte » and « Liste » on `/recherche`) or `aboveBookingBar` (the salon page's bar). A toast with neither prop, every other page's, is never measured and never moves.

**Its home** is where its classes put it, unchanged: `bottom-xxxl pb-m` for « Carte » (80 px, px like the 24–72 px band the button rides in) and `bottom-20` for the bar (5rem: 80 px at normal text, 160 px with the 200 % font, rem like the bar). Both reset to `bottom-l` from `lg`. The pill is centred (`left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2`).

**The controls** are the elements marked `data-floating-control` whose box is on the screen (its foot below the screen's top and its top above the screen's foot). A control that is not displayed (`lg:hidden`, `md:landscape:hidden`) has a 0×0 box at the corner and does not count. The marks are on « Carte », « Liste » and the salon page's bar, and nowhere else.

**When it moves.** Only when the pill at its home would come within `s`, 8 px, of a control: their boxes overlap once the control's is grown by 8 px on every side. Then it moves in this fixed order, which is not « the smallest move »:
1. **Aside**, where the browser has the CSS `translate` property: to the right first, then to the left, each just far enough to leave 8 px between the pill and every control it met. A slide is taken only if (a) the pill stays at least `m`, 16 px, the page's gutter, from that side of the screen; (b) it then clears every control; and (c) it does not newly cover another control's label. **A label** is one box per `a[href]` or `button` on the page: the union of the boxes of its non-blank text (`Range.getBoundingClientRect`), such as a row's ♥ or a link's words, never its tap area. A control with no text, such as a row's photo link, has none; the floating controls, anything inside one, and the toast's own « Annuler » do not count. **Words only a screen reader reads are no part of a label** (after the build's review): text under an absolutely placed, clipped element (`sr-only`), the control itself included, such as « Aller au contenu » until it is focused. Their box is as wide as the words though nothing shows: at 320 a row's link ran 45 px past its visible text. Measured by the review on the built tree: no slide decision changed in six scanned setups; re-measured on the fix, the 34 figures the review read are the same (§2.9). **Newly** means covered by the slid pill and not by the pill at home. Any overlap counts, with no threshold: a glyph partly covered is already misread, and a threshold would be arbitrary. The labels are read only when a slide is weighed, once per placement.
2. **Else up or down, whichever is the shorter move** (the nearer side), up on a tie. A rise ends the pill 8 px above the highest control it met; a drop starts it 8 px below the lowest. A rise is capped so the pill's top stays 16 px from the screen's top, a drop so its foot stays 16 px from the screen's foot.
3. **Where neither clears** (a capped move still within 8 px of a control), the pill takes, among staying, the shorter move and the longer one, in that order, the one that covers the controls least (the height of the pill standing on them, the 8 px of air included); a tie keeps the earlier. So it stays whole on the screen, never nearer an edge than 16 px, where it covers the control least.

**Whole px.** What a move needs is rounded up, so the 8 px of air stay whole; the room that caps a rise or a drop is rounded down, so the 16 px edges stay whole; and a slide is tested as it will be worn. A move that would fit only before rounding is not taken.

**How it is worn.** Straight on the live region's inline style, not through React state, so every frame reads the move the pill really wears: aside as `translate`, which leaves the region's width, and so the pill's wrapping, as it is; up as a positive `margin-bottom`, down as a negative one. Each placement first takes off the move worn, then measures the home. The region stays `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"`, so what is announced does not change. There is no animation: the pill steps, so there is nothing for `prefers-reduced-motion` to stop.

**When it is placed.** Once when the message shows; then on every `scroll` (passive) and `resize`, one `requestAnimationFrame` per burst; on a `ResizeObserver` of the pill and of each marked control; and on a `MutationObserver` of `document.body` (child list, subtree), which re-places and starts watching a control that appears with no scroll, such as « Liste » after « Carte » at the top of the page. When the message goes, or the page unmounts, the move goes, the listeners are removed and the observers disconnected. Each way of being placed again with no scroll has its own guard since the build's review: a resize alone (a unit case and an e2e case), a control that grows (a unit case, through its own `ResizeObserver`), a control that appears (a unit case and E8).

**Older browsers.**
- **No CSS `translate` property** (Chrome before 104, Safari before 14.1, from support tables; `CSS.supports('translate', '1px')` is false): no slide, so the code never believes in a slide the page does not show, and the pill takes the shorter of up and down. Held by a unit case; not scanned in a browser. Their share among the site's visitors is UNVERIFIED.
- **No `ResizeObserver`** (an old WebView, and jsdom): no throw, and the scroll and resize listeners place the pill alone; a control that changes size with no scroll is caught at the next scroll or resize (inferred). Held by two unit cases: no throw and the first placement; and, added after the build's review, a resize alone, with no observer and no scroll, placing the pill again.
- **No `MutationObserver`**: no throw; a control that appears with no scroll is placed against at the next scroll or resize (inferred). Every browser Next 14 supports has it (inferred from support tables).

**Not changed:** the pill's look, its words, `z-toast`, its 10 s life for the heart's message, which carries « Annuler » (decision 16), and `pointer-events-none` on the region, with « Annuler » the one control in it that takes presses.

**Parity.** Flutter's `Scaffold` lifts a floating `SnackBar` above a `FloatingActionButton` and never slides it (framework behaviour, UNVERIFIED in this app's screens). The app has no « Carte » button floating over its list (inferred).
